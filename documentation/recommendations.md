## Sales Recommendations

### 1. Focus marketing spend on peak months
- October, November, December show the highest revenue
- **Action**: Increase inventory and marketing budget 6–8 weeks before Diwali and December

### 2. Weekend-specific promotions
- Weekend transactions show higher average order values
- **Action**: Run exclusive weekend flash sales to boost conversion

### 3. Reduce discount dependency in low-margin categories
- High discount rates in some categories erode profit margin
- **Action**: Introduce minimum margin guardrails before applying discounts

---

## Customer Recommendations

### 4. Win-back "At Risk" and "Lost" customers
- RFM analysis identifies customers who have not purchased recently
- **Action**: Send targeted re-engagement campaigns with personalised offers

### 5. Reward "Champions" and "Loyal Customers"
- Top RFM segments generate disproportionate revenue
- **Action**: Create a loyalty tier programme with exclusive benefits

### 6. Convert "One-Time" buyers to repeat customers
- A large portion of customers make only one purchase
- **Action**: Send a follow-up email within 7 days of first purchase with a relevant product suggestion

---

## Product Recommendations

### 7. Prioritise high-revenue, high-margin categories
- Categories with both high revenue and high margin should receive priority shelf space and promotion
- **Action**: Review planogram and allocate more space to these categories

### 8. Review pricing on low-margin products
- Products with revenue but low margin may be priced incorrectly or over-discounted
- **Action**: Conduct a price review and reduce discount frequency for these SKUs

---

## Inventory Recommendations

### 9. Address high-stockout products
- Products with stockout rates above 10% are likely losing sales
- **Action**: Increase reorder points and safety stock for these products

### 10. Clear slow-moving inventory
- Products with high closing stock and very low daily sales are tying up working capital
- **Action**: Run clearance promotions or bundle them with fast-moving products

---

## Marketing Recommendations

### 11. Shift budget toward highest-response channels
- Response rates vary significantly by channel
- **Action**: Reallocate campaign spend toward channels with the highest response rate

### 12. Personalise campaigns by customer segment
- Different RFM segments respond differently to offers
- **Action**: Send discount-heavy campaigns to At Risk and Lost segments, and exclusive access campaigns to Champions
