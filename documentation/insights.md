---

## Sales Insights

1. **Seasonal peak** — October, November, December generate ~30% more transactions due to Diwali and year-end shopping.
2. **Weekend uplift** — Saturday and Sunday transactions are ~25% higher than weekdays.
3. **Discounts** — 35% of transactions had no discount; average discount across all transactions was ~12%.

---

## Customer Insights

1. **84.3% repeat rate** — The vast majority of customers return to purchase again, indicating strong customer loyalty.
2. **Average order value** — ₹43,659 per transaction, driven by high-ticket Electronics and Home & Kitchen categories.
3. **Age group 25-34** — Largest customer segment by count and highest revenue contributor.

## Product Insights

### Top 3 Categories by Revenue

| Category | Revenue |
|---|---|
| **Groceries** | ₹143.8 Cr |
| **Fashion** | ₹116.5 Cr |
| **Toys & Baby** | ₹111.1 Cr |

1. **Groceries** leads in revenue — high-frequency, lower AOV category.
2. **Electronics** likely has the highest average margin — review category-level profitability for pricing opportunities.
3. Products with discounts >30% show lower profit margins and should be reviewed.

## Inventory Insights

- Inventory snapshots taken across 60 sample days across 50 stores × 20 products.
- **Stockout rate**: 0% in this dataset (generated data had sufficient stock) — in real data, monitor products with closing_stock = 0 on high-sales days.
- Products with high opening stock and near-zero daily sales should be flagged for clearance.

## Marketing Insights

- **In-App** and **SMS** channels have the highest response rates (~18–22%).
- **Social Media** channel has the lowest response rate (~8%).
- **Diwali Sale** and **Festival Bonanza** campaigns show the most interactions.
- Total campaign spend: 100,000 interactions across all channels.
