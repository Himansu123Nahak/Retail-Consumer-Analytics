# Business Requirements

## Project Context

A retail company with stores across multiple regions wants to understand its business performance using transaction, customer, product, store, inventory, and marketing data.

## Business Problem

> "How can a retail company use its data to increase revenue, improve customer retention, optimise inventory, and make better business decisions?"

## Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Interest |
|---|---|
| CEO / Executive Team | Overall revenue, profit, growth |
| Sales Manager | Store and category performance |
| Marketing Manager | Campaign ROI, customer response |
| Inventory Manager | Stock levels, stockouts |
| Customer Success | Retention, repeat customers |

## Business Questions to Answer

### Sales Performance
- What is the total revenue, profit, and profit margin?
- How has revenue trended over months and years?
- Which categories generate the most revenue?
- Which regions and states perform best?
- Which stores are top performers and which are underperforming?
- What is the impact of discounts on revenue?

### Customer Analytics
- How many unique customers do we have?
- What percentage are repeat customers vs one-time buyers?
- Which customer demographics (age, gender, income) spend the most?
- Who are our highest-value customers?
- What is the average customer lifetime value?

### Product Analytics
- Which products sell the most?
- Which products are most profitable?
- Are there products with high revenue but low profit?

### Inventory
- What is the stockout rate across products?
- Which products are slow-moving?

### Marketing
- Which campaign channels have the best response rate?
- Which campaigns drove the most engagement?

## Success Criteria

All KPIs defined in `KPIs.md` should reconcile within 1% across SQL, Excel, and Power BI.
